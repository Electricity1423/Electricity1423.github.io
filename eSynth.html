Lag mode

Delays when MIDI events are heard, to imitate a lagging player. Events are never dropped: late events play as soon as the lag ends, in their original order. Works in live playback and in WAV export.

Looks for moments where the picture stops changing (frozen frames) or updates at a lower rate than the rest of the video (stutter). Each one becomes a lag region you can review before adding.
Assumes the song clock keeps running through each freeze (video time minus “Song starts at” = song time). Quiet parts of a song also look frozen, so review the list; “Ignore empty screens” skips frames with no notes on them.

Sound & performance

Engine

Restarting reloads your SoundFonts and MIDI. Black MIDI is CPU-bound: lower Max voices or use Safe latency if you hear crackling. Use WAV export for the cleanest result.

Render to WAV

Export always uses cubic interpolation and ignores the realtime voice cap.
